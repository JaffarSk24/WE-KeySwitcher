# Dictionary data

The files in this folder (`*.dict`, `*.ngram`) and the word lists in `Tests/data` are generated by `tools/prep_data.py` from:

- [FrequencyWords](https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords) by Hermit Dave, 2018 lists for Russian, English and Slovak, built from [OpenSubtitles](https://www.opensubtitles.org/). Content licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0 (code under MIT).
- [wikipedia-word-frequency](https://github.com/IlyaSemenov/wikipedia-word-frequency) by Ilya Semenov, lists `enwiki-2023-04-13` and `ruwiki-2022-08-29`, built from Wikipedia, whose text is licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0 (code under MIT).

What was changed: words were filtered by alphabet and minimal count, lower-cased, Russian `ё` merged into `е`, Slovak words also stored without diacritics, the subtitle and Wikipedia frequencies mixed (65% and 35%), and everything stored as hashed counts (`*.dict`) and character n-gram counts (`*.ngram`).

These derived files are licensed under the [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International License](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

## Formats

`*.dict`: `WEKD` magic, `UInt32` word count, `UInt64` total count, then the sorted 64-bit FNV-1a hashes of the words (UTF-8, little endian) and their `UInt32` counts in the same order.

`*.ngram`: text, one `gram<TAB>count` per line, grams of 1 to 4 characters, `^` marks the start of a word and `$` its end.
